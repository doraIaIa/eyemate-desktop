Employee always knows when a campaign is active and which aggregate data may be used.

## Allowed Campaign Templates

- Healthy Break Month.
- 20-20-20 Awareness.
- New Employee Screen Setup.
- Peak Workload Recovery.

Templates are bounded by eligible cohort, start/end date, approved reminder intensity, organization introduction text within limits and pause/stop.

## Prohibited Campaign Behavior

- No camera coercion.
- No hidden measurement.
- No leaderboard.
- No personal target.
- No manager individual result.
- No free-form health claims.
- No focus, fatigue, productivity or health score.
- No list of employees who opted out, ignored reminders or skipped breaks.
- No reward or punishment from individual metrics.

## Required Controls

- Employee notice before campaign starts.
- Preview of message, schedule and aggregate fields.
- Employee opt-out and campaign pause/stop.
- Reminder frequency cap and quiet hours.
- One active campaign per cohort.
- Aggregate-only metrics and small cohort suppression.
- Post-campaign survey must be voluntary.
- Campaign deletion/retention and emergency rollback must be defined.
