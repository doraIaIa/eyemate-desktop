## Purpose

EyeMate Program Implementation & Participation Report summarizes deployment period, activated seats, aggregate participation, aggregate engagement, data coverage, campaigns, voluntary employee feedback, methodology version, limitations and authenticity status.

Vietnamese name: **Báo cáo triển khai và mức độ tham gia chương trình EyeMate**.

It is not a legal, ISO, HSE, medical or productivity certificate.

## Allowed Claims

- Program was deployed to defined cohorts during a reporting window.
- Activated seats are reported for the deployment period.
- Aggregate participation, break engagement, campaign reach, data coverage and helpfulness are reported with methodology version.
- Voluntary employee feedback may be reported only as aggregate.
- Some cells may be suppressed due to privacy thresholds.
- Results are observational and do not prove causality.

## Prohibited Claims

- Legal compliance, ISO compliance or HSE compliance.
- Medical effectiveness or employee health status.
- Causal productivity improvement.
- Employee compliance.
- Individual risk, focus, fatigue or productivity claim.

## Inputs And Methodology

Inputs are thresholded cohort aggregates, campaign metadata, data coverage and app/version health. Each report must include methodology version, reporting window, limitations, issue date, correction state and revocation state.

## Authenticity And QR Semantics

QR verification may verify only:

- Issuer.
- Document hash.
- Report ID.
- Issue/revocation state.
- Methodology version.

QR must not verify legal compliance, health outcome, medical effectiveness or productivity improvement.

## Retention And Access

Reports are retained by organization policy. Corrections and revocations must be visible. Export must not bypass privacy gates or reveal suppressed cells.
