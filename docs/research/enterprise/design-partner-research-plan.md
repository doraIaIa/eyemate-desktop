## Participants

- 5 HR/People/EHS participants.
- 5 IT/Security participants.
- 3 Privacy/Legal/DPO participants.
- 8 to 10 employees.
- 2 to 3 budget owners.

## Hypotheses

- Buyers will accept aggregate-only evidence if it is credible and procurement-ready.
- Employees will reject the program if opt-out, no manager drill-down and no camera coercion are not obvious.
- Privacy/Legal will require thresholding, anti-differencing and audit before pilot.
- IT/Security will require clear separation between Control Plane and Insights Plane.

## Recruitment Criteria

Screen for organizations with screen-heavy work, existing wellbeing interest and privacy review capacity. Exclude prospects who require individual productivity or health monitoring.

Qualified design partner criteria:

- at least about 50 screen-based employees;
- named HR/EHS sponsor;
- named IT/Security contact;
- agreement to employee transparency;
- no requirement for individual monitoring;
- agreement not to use data for performance management;
- willingness to provide aggregate feedback;
- acceptance of pilot success/failure criteria.

## Interview Guide

- What workplace visual wellbeing problem is worth solving?
- Which aggregate signals would be useful without identifying employees?
- What would make this unacceptable?
- What evidence is needed for procurement?
- What employee transparency wording builds trust?
- What would you pay for if no individual monitoring is included?

## Evidence Capture

Capture notes with consent, role, company segment and objection tags. Do not store sensitive employee health stories unless explicitly consented and minimized.

## Decision Rules

Proceed to technical planning only if all of the following are true:

- at least 12 valid interviews across HR/EHS, IT/Security, Privacy/Legal, employees and budget owners;
- at least 2 qualified design partners;
- the same job-to-be-done is validated by at least 2 buyers;
- no unresolved privacy principle blocker remains;
- metric dictionary is stable enough for pilot;
- threat controls are assessed as feasible.

Proceed to implementation only after at least one written pilot agreement or LOI, project-owner technical-plan approval, Security/Privacy pilot architecture approval and pilot-ready personal EyeMate capability.

## Invalidation Criteria

- Buyers require individual monitoring.
- Employees believe opt-out is not real.
- Privacy/Legal rejects aggregate relay concept.
- IT/Security rejects Control Plane metadata scope.

Prototype praise alone is not evidence of product-market fit.
