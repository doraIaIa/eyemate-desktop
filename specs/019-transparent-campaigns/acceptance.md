## Positive Acceptance

- `AC-ENT-CAM-001`: Given campaign creation, employee preview shows message, schedule, aggregate fields and opt-out.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-002`: Given campaign pause, future campaign reminders stop.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-003`: Given post-campaign survey, helpfulness is voluntary and aggregate.

## Negative Acceptance

- `AC-ENT-CAM-101`: Campaign cannot be hidden from employee.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-102`: Camera cannot be forced on.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-103`: No leaderboard can be configured.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-104`: No personal target can be configured.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-105`: Manager cannot see individual campaign result.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-106`: Opt-out identity is not exposed.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-107`: Reward, punishment, discipline or performance action from individual metrics is rejected.
- `AC-ENT-CAM-108`: Free-form medical or health claim is rejected.
